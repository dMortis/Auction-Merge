Created with the built-in image generation tool. Final PNGs are copied directly from its native output, without resampling. The original concept remains in Art/Concept. The previous background containing UI is archived in the source folder at Backgrounds/Archive/Auction_Merge_Background_WithUI.png.

- Art/UI/Collections/CollectionCard_Background.png — 997×1578. Empty reusable parchment card with ornate gold frame and blank lower plaque. Transparency outside the frame; no characters, progress indicators or text.

Unity import: Sprite, Full Rect, bilinear, clamp, no mipmaps, uncompressed, max size 4096. This avoids introducing block-compression artifacts; the native raster resolution is listed above and is not advertised as 4K. Generative edits preserve the visual direction but are not pixel-identical to the source concept.

Inside it place EXACTLY FOUR COLUMNS AND FIVE ROWS of twenty identical LARGE SQUARE empty cream parchment tiles. Board centered horizontally, approximately centered at 46 percent of canvas height from the top; outer board width approximately 67 percent of full expanded canvas width, outer board height approximately 52 percent of canvas height. Highest available native detail; no blur, stretched texture or artificial sharpening.

The artwork has small hand-painted edge variations. The runtime and editor-generated interaction rectangles use identical fixed sizes and evenly spaced centers without any row-dependent perspective scaling.
